# Leah

> Leah (formerly ContractPodAi) is an enterprise agentic AI platform that unifies contract lifecycle management (CLM), legal, procurement, and finance operations into one connected system for the commercial enterprise.

Leah is a ContractPod Technologies Limited company. ContractPodAi rebranded to Leah: the same company, the same legal entity, and the same products under the Leah name. The canonical domain is https://leahai.com, and all pages are served as standard HTML; no separate Markdown versions are published. Leah has been recognized as a Gartner CLM Visionary five times and is SOC 2 Type II certified. This file is a curated guide to the most authoritative public pages on leahai.com. For exhaustive URL discovery, use the XML sitemap linked in the Optional section.
